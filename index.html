Ask a question ↗
What should I wear?

Wear whatever feels comfortable. The spa team will share any treatment-specific preparation details when confirming your visit.

Which treatment is right for me?

Choose a starting point above, or select “Help me choose” in the enquiry form and describe what you are looking for.

Can I enquire for two?

Yes. Choose Couples Retreat in the form and mention any timing preferences. Availability is confirmed by the spa team.

Is my appointment confirmed online?

No. The form prepares an email enquiry only. Your visit is not confirmed until you send the email and receive a reply from the spa.
